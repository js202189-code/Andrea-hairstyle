About Andrea

Hello, my name is Andrea.

Booking & Fees

Here's how securing your appointment works, and a few extra charges that can apply.

How to book your appointment

  • Confirm your date and how many additional people (if any) will be included in the service.
  • Once availability is confirmed, a deposit secures your appointment on the schedule.
  • Quinceañera deposit: $75
  • Family & friends deposit: $50
  • Accepted payment methods: Zelle, Cash App, Apple Pay, Cash
Send your deposit to (915) 251-9682 — Andrea Bencomo. Reach out if you need help with any payment method.

Additional fees

  • Hair extensions application: +$20 (client provides their own extensions)
  • Extra-long hair (past the reference length shown): +$20
  • Arriving with wet hair: +$25
  • More than 30 minutes late to your scheduled appointment: +$30, out of respect for the artist's time

Cancellation Policy

01

The deposit is non-refundable under any circumstances.

02

In the event of a cancellation, please inform Andrea with at least 30 days' notice.

03

A reschedule can save your deposit, but it's subject to availability.

04

Once the number of services is confirmed, those spots are reserved and blocked on the schedule.

05

If anyone doesn't show up on the day of the event, the contract holder is responsible for the full cost of that service, since the time slot was reserved exclusively.

06

In extraordinary, verifiable emergencies, rescheduling will be considered, always subject to availability.

By submitting a deposit and confirming your appointment, you agree to all the policies listed above.
